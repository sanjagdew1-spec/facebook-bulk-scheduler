Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro is a desktop software application that helps users manage and schedule content for Facebook Pages.

Information We Access

When you connect your Facebook account, the application may access information required to display and manage Facebook Pages that you are authorized to manage.

This may include Facebook Page information, Page identifiers, access permissions, and information required to publish or schedule content.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Facebook is used only to provide the application's Facebook Page management and publishing features.

Facebook Data

We do not sell Facebook user data. We do not use Facebook data for purposes unrelated to providing the requested application features.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. Users should keep their Facebook credentials, access tokens, and other account information secure.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro by contacting the application owner.

For data deletion requests, please contact the application owner through the contact method provided with the application.
