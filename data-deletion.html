User Data Deletion

Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro

Users can request deletion of information associated with their use of Facebook Bulk Scheduler Pro.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact the application owner and provide the Facebook account or Page information associated with your use of the application.

What Happens After a Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, the application owner will review the request and delete applicable information associated with the user, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes.

Facebook Access

Users can also remove the application's Facebook permissions through their Facebook account settings.

Contact

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, please contact the application owner through the contact method provided with the application.
